How to use Hand Lab

Getting started

  1. Allow camera access when the browser asks.
  2. Hold your hand about an arm’s length from the camera, in good light, palm facing the screen.
  3. Pick a mode with the tabs at the top, or press 1 2 3.

  • The outer ring shows confidence: grey = no letter, amber = unsure, green = will type.
  • J: make an I and draw a hook with your little finger. Z: point and draw a Z in the air. Start moving within about 0.8 s.
  • G and H point sideways, P and Q point down.
  • Rules knows 19 letters. Record samples in Train, then switch to k-NN or MLP for all 24 static letters.

Evaluate classifiers

Random mixes neighbouring, nearly identical frames into both train and test, so it overestimates accuracy. Time-blocked holds out the last 20% of each recording, which is closer to a fresh attempt. Leave-one-signer-out (2+ signers) tests on people the model has never seen.
